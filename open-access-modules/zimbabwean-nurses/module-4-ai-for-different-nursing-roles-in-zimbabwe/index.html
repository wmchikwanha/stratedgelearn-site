AI for Zimbabwean Nurses

Module 4: AI for Different Nursing Roles in Zimbabwe

AI for Different Nursing Roles in Zimbabwe (this module offers some examples of nursing roles, the rest of which can be found in the online ebook)

Enrolled Nurse (EN) & Registered General Nurse (RGN)

As the frontline of care, ENs and RGNs handle a vast range of tasks. AI can be a great support for:

  • Drafting Patient Education Materials:
    • Prompt Example: "Draft simple hygiene tips in Shona for primary school children to prevent worms, focusing on handwashing and wearing shoes." (Remember to get this verified for accurate Shona).
    • AI Benefit: Helps create locally relevant, simple health messages quickly.
  • Summarising Data for Handover Notes:
    • Prompt Example (after typing in a list of anonymised observations): "Summarise these patient observations from my shift into 3 brief bullet points for handover: Patient A - stable, vitals normal. Patient B - reported mild pain, given paracetamol, pain reduced. Patient C - family visited, expressed concerns about discharge plan."
    • AI Benefit: Helps organise information concisely for clearer communication.
  • Creating Checklists for Routine Procedures (Personal Reminder):
    • Prompt Example: "Create a simple checklist of steps for preparing a patient for a basic wound dressing procedure in a clinic setting."
    • AI Benefit: Acts as a personal memory aid to ensure consistency (always follow official protocols).

The general nurses’ diploma qualification produces the majority of nursing graduates in Zimbabwe (PMC - A Call to Rethink Nursing Education in Zimbabwe), making AI tools that support their wide scope of practice particularly valuable.

Advanced Practice Nurse (APN)

APNs often deal with more complex cases and may have mentorship roles. AI can assist with:

  • Summarising Research Articles:
    • Prompt Example: "Summarise the key findings and implications of this research article abstract on [topic, e.g., new diabetes management techniques]: [paste abstract text]."
    • AI Benefit: Helps stay updated with new evidence more efficiently. Identifying research gaps, for example, for student projects, also becomes easier.
  • Drafting Complex Care Plan Templates:
    • Prompt Example: "Outline a template for a comprehensive nursing care plan for a patient with chronic heart failure, including sections for assessment, diagnosis, interventions, and evaluation, tailored for community follow-up."
    • AI Benefit: Provides a structured starting point for developing detailed care plans.
  • Exploring Differential Diagnosis Ideas (as a thought-starter, not a diagnostic tool):
    • Prompt Example: "A 60-year-old male patient in rural Zimbabwe presents with chronic cough, weight loss, and night sweats. What are some potential differential diagnoses to consider, keeping in mind common conditions in the region?"
    • AI Benefit: Can help brainstorm possibilities to consider alongside clinical expertise and diagnostic tests. Never use for actual diagnosis.

APNs in Africa are crucial for primary healthcare, and tools that support their advanced skills are beneficial (Wiley Online Library - Advanced practice nursing initiatives in Africa).

Nurse Educator

Educators shape the next generation of nurses. AI can be a useful teaching aide:

  • Creating Quiz Questions or Case Study Scenarios:
    • Prompt Example: "Generate 3 multiple-choice quiz questions about the side effects of Metformin for nursing students." OR "Create a short case study scenario for a nursing student about a child presenting with dehydration in a Zimbabwean clinic. Include vital signs and social context."
    • AI Benefit: Saves time in developing teaching materials.
  • Drafting Lesson Plan Outlines:
    • Prompt Example: "Outline a lesson plan for a 1-hour session on basic infection prevention and control for first-year nursing diploma students in Zimbabwe."
    • AI Benefit: Provides a structure for lesson planning.
  • Generating Feedback Templates:
    • Prompt Example: "Create a feedback template for assessing a nursing student's communication skills during a simulated patient interaction. Include sections for positive points and areas for improvement."
    • AI Benefit: Helps standardise and streamline feedback.

Nursing education in Zimbabwe has various programmes (PMC - A Call to Rethink Nursing Education in Zimbabwe), and AI can support educators across these.

Nurse Manager

Managers oversee operations and support their teams. AI can assist with:

  • Drafting Communications to Staff:
    • Prompt Example: "Draft a polite and encouraging memo to nursing staff about an upcoming team meeting to discuss improving patient satisfaction scores. Mention date, time, and location."
    • AI Benefit: Helps create clear and professional communications quickly.
  • Brainstorming Solutions for Common Ward/Clinic Challenges:
    • Prompt Example: "Suggest 3 practical and low-cost ways to improve medicine storage and organisation in a small, busy clinic with limited shelf space."
    • AI Benefit: Can provide fresh ideas or perspectives on operational issues.
