Professional Registration Requirements: The use of artificial intelligence tools in engineering practice does not diminish the requirement for professional registration, continuing professional development, or adherence to professional codes of conduct. AI Output Reliability: Artificial intelligence systems, including large language models, are not infallible and may produce inaccurate, incomplete, or misleading information. All AI-generated calculations, analyses, designs, and recommendations must be thoroughly reviewed, validated, and verified by qualified engineering professionals before implementation. The accuracy of AI outputs can vary significantly depending on input quality, prompt formulation, and the specific AI system used.

Technical Validation Required: Every calculation, analysis, or design recommendation generated using AI tools must be independently verified using established engineering principles, relevant standards, and professional judgment. AI tools should be considered as sophisticated calculators or design aids, not as substitutes for professional engineering expertise and validation.
