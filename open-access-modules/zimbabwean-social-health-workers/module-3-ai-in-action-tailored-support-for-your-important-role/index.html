AI for Zimbabwean Social Health Workers

Module 3: AI in Action - Tailored Support for Your Important Role

This module offers some examples of social worker roles, the rest of which can be found in the online ebook)

In this chapter, we will look at how AI can specifically help different types of Social Health Workers in Zimbabwe. For each role, we'll explore some common tasks and show practical examples of how ChatGPT, Microsoft Copilot, or Google Gemini can lend a hand. Remember, AI is your assistant, helping you be even more effective in your vital work!

The examples are divided into:

  • Basic User: Simple, direct questions to AI.
  • Mid-Level User: More detailed requests or refining AI's output.
  • Advanced User (still simple): Using AI for slightly more complex tasks like structuring information or drafting more detailed content.

1. Youth Advocate

Your Role and Its Vital Work:

As a Youth Advocate in Zimbabwe, you play a crucial role in empowering adolescents and young people. Your work often involves community engagement, running peer education sessions, advocating for youth rights, and disseminating information on important topics like Sexual and Reproductive Health (SRH), HIV prevention, COVID-19 awareness, and substance abuse prevention (UNICEF Zimbabwe, Zvandiri Advocacy). You act as an ambassador, enhancing youth engagement and addressing drivers of risk.

How Your AI Companion Can Lend a Hand:

Task: Preparing for a workshop on SRH for teenagers.

  • Basic User Prompt: "ChatGPT, what are 3 important topics to cover in an SRH talk for 15-year-olds in Zimbabwe?"
  • Mid-Level User Prompt: "Copilot, help me outline a 1-hour interactive workshop for youth (14-18 years) on making healthy choices about relationships and SRH. Suggest one icebreaker activity."
  • Advanced User Prompt: "Gemini, I have notes about consent, STIs, and contraception. Can you help me structure these into a simple, engaging presentation format for young people? For each topic, suggest a key message and a discussion question."

Task: Drafting an announcement for a youth club meeting.

  • Basic User Prompt: "ChatGPT, write a short announcement for a youth club meeting about mental wellness."
  • Mid-Level User Prompt: "Copilot, draft a catchy WhatsApp message to invite youth in Harare to a meeting next Saturday at 2 PM at the community hall to discuss challenges young people face and plan a local campaign. Make it sound exciting."

Task: Finding information on youth rights.

  • Basic User Prompt: "Gemini, what are some general rights of a child or young person?" (Always verify with official Zimbabwean legal documents or child rights organisations).

Task: Getting ideas for a social media post about substance abuse prevention.

  • Mid-Level User Prompt: "ChatGPT, give me 3 ideas for short, impactful social media posts for Instagram or Facebook targeting Zimbabwean youth about the dangers of drug abuse. Suggest a relevant hashtag."

2. Lay Counsellor / Mental Health Worker

Your Role and Its Vital Work:

As a Lay Counsellor or Mental Health Worker in Zimbabwe, often working in innovative programmes like the "Friendship Bench," your role is vital in bridging the mental health gap. You provide basic counselling, support individuals with common mental disorders (CMDs) like anxiety and depression using techniques such as problem-solving therapy, create trusting alliances with clients, offer psycho-social support, and make referrals when needed (T&F Online: Experiences of Counsellors with Friendship Bench, PMC: Problem-solving therapy by lay health workers). Challenges can include combating stigma around mental illness and needing ongoing supervision.

How Your AI Companion Can Lend a Hand:

Task: Finding information on coping strategies.

  • Basic User Prompt: "ChatGPT, what are simple coping strategies for stress that I can share with someone?"
  • Mid-Level User Prompt: "Copilot, list 5 relaxation techniques that are easy to explain and practice, suitable for someone feeling anxious in a low-resource setting."

Task: Preparing simple educational material on mental health.

  • Mid-Level User Prompt: "Gemini, help me write a short, easy-to-understand paragraph explaining what anxiety is, for a community awareness talk in Zimbabwe. Use simple language."
  • Advanced User Prompt: "ChatGPT, I want to create a simple leaflet about the signs of depression in adolescents. Can you provide 3-4 key signs and for each, a supportive message for parents or friends? Keep it culturally sensitive for Zimbabwe."

Task: Planning a group support session.

  • Advanced User Prompt: "Copilot, I need to plan a group support session on positive coping mechanisms for adults. Can you suggest 2 simple activities and a brief way to explain the benefit of each activity?"

Task: Understanding referral pathways (general information).

  • Basic User Prompt: "Gemini, what kind of support can a primary health clinic typically offer for mental health concerns?" (Reminder: Actual referral pathways are local and specific; AI provides general info only).

3. Faith-Based Health Worker

Your Role and Its Vital Work:

As a Faith-Based Health Worker, you often operate through Faith-Based Organisations (FBOs) which play an increasingly momentous role in providing healthcare services in Zimbabwe, especially when state facilities are challenged (Scielo SA: Role of FBOs in Zimbabwe health). Your tasks can include direct health service provision within your faith community, health education, offering psycho-social and spiritual support, and responding to health crises. This work provides opportunities for evangelism but can also strain FBO resources.

How Your AI Companion Can Lend a Hand:

Task: Preparing a health talk for your congregation.

  • Basic User Prompt: "ChatGPT, give me 3 key health messages about healthy eating that I can share with my church group."
  • Mid-Level User Prompt: "Copilot, help me draft a short talk (about 5 minutes) for a faith community on the importance of hygiene for preventing diseases, linking it to principles of cleanliness in faith."

Task: Finding information about a common health issue affecting the community.

  • Basic User Prompt: "Gemini, what are common symptoms of high blood pressure?" (Always advise seeking medical diagnosis).
  • Advanced User Prompt: "ChatGPT, I need to explain diabetes simply to an elderly group in my community. Can you help me create an analogy or simple story to explain what happens in the body? Ensure it's respectful and easy to grasp."

Task: Drafting a message of support for a family affected by illness.

  • Mid-Level User Prompt: "Copilot, help me write a short, comforting message for a family in our faith community who is caring for a sick loved one. Include a message of hope."

Task: Organizing a health awareness event.

  • Advanced User Prompt: "Gemini, I'm helping organize a health screening day at our church. Can you help me list key tasks to consider, like inviting health professionals, informing the community, and arranging the space?"

4. Environmental Health Technician (EHT)

Your Role and Its Vital Work:

As an Environmental Health Technician (EHT) in Zimbabwe, your work is critical for public health. You conduct inspections of trading and non-trading premises, carry out disease surveillance, manage water points and sanitation projects, promote hygiene, and are involved in food safety (Bulawayo City Council EHT Vacancy, FAO Environmental Health Curriculum). EHTs were also active in COVID-19 early detection (WHO Africa on EHTs & COVID-19). Your role requires good communication and teamwork.

How Your AI Companion Can Lend a Hand:

Task: Preparing for a food market inspection.

  • Basic User Prompt: "ChatGPT, what are general guidelines for safe food handling in markets?" (Disclaimer: Always refer to official MoHCC/local authority food safety regulations).
  • Mid-Level User Prompt: "Copilot, create a simple checklist of 5 key things to look for during a basic hygiene inspection of a food stall."

Task: Drafting notes for an inspection report.

  • Advanced User Prompt: "Gemini, I observed the following at a site: 'poor waste disposal near water source, evidence of rodents, no handwashing facility for staff.' Help me phrase these points professionally for an inspection report summary."

Task: Creating health promotion material on sanitation.

  • Mid-Level User Prompt: "ChatGPT, draft 3 key messages about preventing cholera through safe water and sanitation, suitable for a community health talk in a rural Zimbabwean village."
  • Advanced User Prompt: "Copilot, I need to design a poster about proper latrine use. Suggest 2 simple visuals and a short, catchy slogan in Shona or Ndebele (and English translation) to promote this."

Task: Researching information on controlling a specific vector (e.g., mosquitoes).

  • Basic User Prompt: "Gemini, what are common community-based methods for mosquito control to prevent malaria?"
