Electrical Work Safety: Electrical work can be lethal. Always follow Zimbabwe Electricity Supply Authority (ZESA) regulations, municipal codes, and international safety standards. Switch off power supplies, use proper testing equipment, and never work beyond your certified competence level.

Structural and Mechanical Safety: Welding, construction, and mechanical work can cause serious injury or death. Follow all applicable Zimbabwean building codes, occupational health and safety regulations, and industry best practices regardless of AI suggestions.

SPECIFIC TRADES DISCLAIMERS

Electrical Work: Electrical installations must comply with ZESA regulations, IEC standards, and local municipal codes. Improper electrical work can cause fires, electrocution, or death. Always use qualified electricians for complex installations and obtain necessary certificates of compliance.

Plumbing and Water Systems: Water system installations must meet municipal health standards and building codes. Consider water quality, pressure requirements, and contamination risks. Failed water systems can cause property damage and health hazards.

HVAC and Refrigeration: Refrigerant handling requires appropriate certification. Improper installation can cause environmental damage and safety hazards. Follow ozone depletion regulations and refrigerant recovery requirements.

Automotive Work: Vehicle modifications must comply with Vehicle Inspection Department (VID) requirements. Improper automotive work can cause accidents, injuries, or death. Maintain appropriate workshop insurance and certifications.

Welding and Fabrication: Structural welding must meet relevant codes and standards. Inadequate welding can cause catastrophic structural failures. Obtain necessary approvals for structural work and maintain welding certifications.

Construction Work: Building work must comply with municipal building codes and obtain necessary approvals. Structural failures can cause injury, death, and property damage. Engage qualified structural engineers for design work.
