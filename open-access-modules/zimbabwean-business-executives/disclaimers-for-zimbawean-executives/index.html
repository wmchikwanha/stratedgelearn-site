  • AI assistance does not diminish your fiduciary duties or legal obligations as an executive

ARTIFICIAL INTELLIGENCE TECHNOLOGY LIMITATIONS

Fundamental AI Limitations: All AI systems referenced in this handbook have significant constraints:

Technical Limitations:

  • AI responses are based on training data with inherent biases and gaps
  • AI cannot account for real-time market conditions or organisational nuances
  • AI analysis may contain factual errors, logical flaws, or inappropriate recommendations
  • AI systems cannot understand complex organisational politics or cultural dynamics
  • AI outputs require human interpretation and contextualisation

Strategic Decision-Making Limitations:

  • AI cannot replace human intuition, ethical reasoning, or stakeholder relationship management
  • AI analysis may miss critical qualitative factors affecting business decisions
  • AI recommendations may not align with organisational values or long-term strategic objectives
  • AI systems cannot assess reputational risks or stakeholder sentiment accurately

Data Security and Confidentiality Risks:

  • AI platforms may store, analyse, or inadvertently expose confidential business information
  • Proprietary strategic information should never be input into external AI systems
  • Compliance with data protection regulations remains your responsibility
  • Third-party AI providers may have access to your organisational data

  • Medicines Control Authority of Zimbabwe (MCAZ) for relevant sectors

Industry-Specific Compliance: Executives in regulated industries must ensure:

  • Banking: Compliance with Banking Act and prudential requirements
  • Mining: Adherence to Mines and Minerals Act and environmental regulations
  • Manufacturing: Compliance with Standards Association of Zimbabwe requirements
  • Import/Export: Adherence to international trade regulations and sanctions compliance

Professional Standards: Maintain compliance with professional body requirements:

  • Institute of Directors Zimbabwe (IoD Zimbabwe)
  • Confederation of Zimbabwe Industries (CZI) ethical standards
  • Relevant professional accounting, legal, or technical body requirements
